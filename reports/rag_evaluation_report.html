How to Read This Report

Two RAGAS metrics are evaluated here. Both are scored 0–1 (higher is better). Together they capture two complementary failure modes: the LLM answering off-topic and the retrieval pipeline missing relevant content.

📊 Answer Relevancy (AR)
≈ cosine_sim(original_question, synthetic_questions_from_answer)
Measures whether the generated answer actually addresses the question. The judge LLM generates several questions that the answer could be a response to, then compares them to the original question via embeddings.

≥ 0.70 Good 0.50–0.69 Acceptable < 0.50 Off-topic

What affects it: the LLM's ability to stay on-topic and produce coherent answers. Not a measure of factual accuracy.
🔍 Context Recall (CR)
≈ fraction of ground-truth sentences supported by retrieved contexts
Measures whether the retrieval pipeline found the right chunks. The judge LLM checks each sentence of the reference answer to see if it is covered by the retrieved contexts.

≥ 0.50 Good 0.30–0.49 Acceptable < 0.30 Retrieval gap

What affects it: embedding model quality, chunk size, and k (number of retrieved chunks). Low CR = the retriever is missing information the answer needs.

Key Observations & Recommendations

  • Context Recall is the main bottleneck — overall avg CR is 0.2383, well below the 0.50 target. This means the retriever is consistently failing to surface all the relevant information the answer needs, regardless of which LLM is used. Focus optimisation effort here first.
  • Best embedding model: bge-m3 (CR: 0.4225, AR: 0.7616) — clear winner. Its 1024-dim multilingual representation is well-suited to Spanish-language poverty and social program content. Worst: snowflake-arctic-embed (CR: 0.0742) — poor recall for this corpus.
  • Best chunk size: large (2048 chars) (CR: 0.3005) — medium chunks consistently outperform both small and large. Small chunks (512) split context too aggressively, losing coherent passages. Large chunks (2048) add noise and dilute semantic precision in embedding space.
  • Best LLM for answer quality: qwen3:8b (AR: 0.7650) — produces the most on-topic answers. Best LLM for context recall: llama3.2:3b (CR: 0.2432) — better at incorporating retrieved context into its answers.
  • Recommended combination: qwen3:8b + bge-m3 + medium — scores AR: —, CR: —, Avg: 0.6656. Outperforms all other evaluated combinations.
  • Document format matters (slightly): Markdown-formatted chunks outperform plaintext on both metrics (CR +0.0000). Structured headings and lists help the embedding model identify semantic boundaries and retrieve more targeted passages.
  • To improve CR further: consider increasing k_retrieved (currently 8), adding a re-ranking step after retrieval, or experimenting with hybrid search (dense + BM25). The gap between AR (~0.71) and CR (~0.22) suggests a retrieval problem, not an LLM problem.
